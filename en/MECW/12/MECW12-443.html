The Weekly Times, from its point of view, sums up the situation in the following terms:

“The quarter loaf is a shilling—the weather is worse than it has been for half a century at this season of the year—the operative classes are in the delirium of strikes—Asiatic cholera is raging among us once more, and we have got a war mania. We only want war taxes and famine to make up the orthodox number of the plagues of England.”1
Written on November 1, 1853
First published in the New-York Daily Tribune, No. 3925, November 15, 1853
Signed: Karl Marx
